Construction Steel

Reinforcing Steel for Strong Concrete

Deformed bars (rebar) have raised ribs along their length that grip the concrete, so the steel and concrete work together to carry loads. They are used in foundations, columns, beams, slabs and walls. Reinforcing mesh and construction mesh are welded grids that speed up slab and wall work and help control cracking.

Azzar supplies deformed bars, reinforcing mesh and construction mesh to builders, contractors and home owners from our yard in Graniteside, Harare, with delivery arranged according to your location. Always follow your engineer’s or builder’s specification for bar sizes and spacing.

FAQ

Questions Before You Order

Deformed bars reinforce concrete in foundations, columns, beams, slabs and walls. The ribs on the bar grip the concrete so both materials share the load.
Deformed bars are individual reinforcing rods that are tied in place. Reinforcing mesh is a ready-welded grid of steel that is quicker to lay for slabs, floors and some walls.
