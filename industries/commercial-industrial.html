Overview

What This Sector Needs From a Fence

Commercial and industrial sites need fencing that controls access, protects stock and equipment, and still looks professional to customers and visitors.

Azzar provides clearview fencing, diamond mesh and razor wire for warehouses, factories, depots, business parks and yards in Harare and across Zimbabwe, together with gate fabrication and security upgrades.
