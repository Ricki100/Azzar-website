Overview

What This Sector Needs From a Fence

A good home fence protects your family and property, keeps pets and children safe and adds to the look of your home.

Azzar installs clearview fencing, diamond mesh, razor wire toppings and matching gates for houses, townhouse complexes, estates and garden boundaries in Harare and across Zimbabwe.

Appearance

Home owners want a fence that looks neat and suits the house.

Visibility

See-through fencing keeps a view of the street and garden.

Layering

A fence works best with gates, lighting, alarms and other security layers.
