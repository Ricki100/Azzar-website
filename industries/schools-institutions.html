Overview

What This Sector Needs From a Fence

Schools, clinics and public facilities need fencing that keeps learners and patients safe, controls who comes in and still feels open and welcoming.

Azzar provides clearview and diamond mesh fencing, gates and security upgrades for schools, colleges, clinics, hospitals and government facilities in Harare and across Zimbabwe.

Common Challenges

Problems We Help Solve

The fence must stop children leaving unsupervised and keep strangers out.

Controlled access

Gates need to manage drop-off, staff, visitors and deliveries.

Visibility

See-through fencing lets staff supervise grounds and playing fields.

Durability

Fences on busy sites must stand up to daily wear.
