## Important visual distinctions

- ClearVue is rigid welded panel fencing; diamond mesh is flexible woven chain-link.
- Field fence uses rectangular graduated openings and knotted intersections; it is not diamond mesh.
- Construction and reinforcing mesh are welded steel grids for concrete work, not perimeter-security fencing.
- Razor wire has continuous sharp blade-like tape; barbed wire uses spaced barbs twisted onto strands.
- An electric fence is identified by insulated, tensioned live conductors and energiser-related hardware; ordinary barbed strands are not automatically electric.
- A gate should be classified by both its frame/function and its infill, for example “double-leaf diamond-mesh swing gate.”
